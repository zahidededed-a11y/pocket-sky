# POCKET SKY — YOUTUBE SERIES PROJECT PACK

## Instructions for Claude

Use this document as the project brief. Help develop an original, family-friendly manga-inspired animated series for YouTube.

- Main dialogue language: natural, easy-to-understand English.
- Subtitle language: natural Turkish.
- Audience: children and teens, approximately ages 9–16.
- Tone: colorful, funny, adventurous, warm, and exciting.
- Avoid horror, gore, graphic violence, cruelty, adult themes, and gloomy storytelling.
- Create original characters, names, places, stories, and designs. Do not copy existing manga, anime, films, games, or copyrighted characters.
- Keep continuity with the details in this document. Suggest changes before making major changes.
- For future episodes, provide a scene-by-scene English script and a separate, accurately timed Turkish `.srt` subtitle file.
- Make each episode enjoyable on its own, while ending with a cheerful, age-appropriate clue or reveal that makes viewers curious about the next episode.
- Suspense should come from friendship, funny discoveries, puzzles, and surprising reveals—not fear.

---

## Series Overview

### Title
**Pocket Sky**

### Premise
Mina and Theo discover a mysterious map that reveals secret places above their colorful city. With the help of Piko, a curious little cloud-fox, they follow clues, solve playful puzzles, and make new friends.

### Audience
Children and teens, approximately ages 9–16.

### Language and format
- Dialogue: English
- Subtitles: Turkish
- Format: Manga-inspired animated YouTube episodes
- Pilot target length: approximately 5–6 minutes, depending on pacing and visuals

### Tone and themes
Bright, funny, adventurous, heartfelt, and family-friendly. Themes include friendship, curiosity, teamwork, and problem-solving.

---

## Main Characters

### Mina
- Age: 13
- Curious and inventive
- Loves drawing and making maps
- Sometimes acts before she has all the clues
- Carries a sketchbook

### Theo
- Age: 14
- Mina’s thoughtful friend
- Good at puzzles and noticing details
- Makes silly jokes when he is excited or nervous
- Likes folding paper planes

### Piko
- A tiny, fluffy cloud-fox
- Curious, playful, and expressive
- Makes small puffs of wind when excited
- Communicates mostly through sounds, gestures, and facial expressions

## Pilot Episode

### Episode title
**The Map That Sneezed**

### Episode summary
Mina, Theo, and Piko find a mysterious map hidden inside a paper plane. The map seems to sneeze, points them toward a puzzle in the town square, and reveals a tiny floating island above their neighborhood.

## Pilot Script

### Scene 1 — The Paper Plane

**Visual:** A sunny afternoon in a colorful neighborhood. Mina sketches the rooftops from a park bench. Theo tries to fold a paper plane. Piko sits beside them, watching the plane very seriously.

**SFX:** *FOLD. FOLD. FLAP!*

**THEO:** Behold! My fastest paper plane yet.

**MINA:** You said that about the last one.

**THEO:** That one was fast. It just chose the wrong direction.

Theo throws the plane. A tiny breeze from Piko sends it soaring over the park.

**SFX:** *WHOOSH!*

**THEO:** Piko! Did you give it a boost?

Piko makes an innocent face. A soft puff of cloud pops above its head.

**MINA:** I think that means “maybe.”

The plane loops around and lands neatly in Mina’s open sketchbook. A folded piece of paper is tucked inside it.

---

### Scene 2 — A Map Appears

Mina carefully opens the paper. It looks blank at first. Then blue lines appear, drawing the neighborhood—and a tiny island in the sky above the old clock tower.

**SFX:** *TWINKLE!*

**THEO:** That is either a map… or the world’s most confusing doodle.

**MINA:** Look! It’s our neighborhood.

Mina turns her sketchbook around. The drawing matches the streets below them.

**THEO:** And that little island is floating above the clock tower.

Piko leans closer. The map makes a tiny sound.

**SFX:** *ACHOO!*

A little cloud puff lifts the paper into the air. It spins once and lands on Theo’s head.

**THEO:** The map sneezed.

**MINA:** Maps don’t sneeze.

Piko gives another tiny puff. The map flips over and reveals a glowing arrow pointing toward the clock tower.

**MINA:** This one might.

---

### Scene 3 — The Clue

Mina, Theo, and Piko reach the clock tower plaza. They look up. There is no island in sight—only blue sky and a few soft clouds.

**THEO:** Maybe the map is pointing to the top of the tower?

**MINA:** The arrow points past it. See?

Mina holds up the map. The arrow shifts slightly when she turns it toward a small rooftop garden nearby.

They follow the arrow to a planter filled with colorful flowers. A little brass button is hidden beneath one of the flowerpots.

**THEO:** A secret button. I knew this day would become interesting.

**MINA:** Should we press it?

**THEO:** I vote yes. Piko?

Piko taps the button with one paw.

**SFX:** *CLICK!*

A nearby wind chime rings. Its notes make the map’s blue lines shine. The lines form a simple picture: three stars, a moon, and a smiling face.

**MINA:** It’s a pattern.

**THEO:** Three stars, then the moon. What could that mean?

Piko points its nose toward three star-shaped tiles in the plaza. Mina steps on them in order. Theo points to a crescent-shaped tile nearby.

**SFX:** *DING! DING! DING! DONG!*

A small round door opens in the side of the planter. Inside is a bright, silver-colored seed.

---

### Scene 4 — A Tiny Discovery

Mina gently lifts the seed. It gives off a warm glow—not hot, just like sunshine.

**MINA:** There’s something inside it.

**THEO:** Is it a tiny treasure?

The seed gives a small, cheerful wiggle.

**SFX:** *BOOP!*

Piko’s ears pop up. A little sprout appears from the seed, curling into the shape of an arrow.

The arrow points up.

The trio looks toward the sky. A small, fluffy island drifts out from behind a cloud. Flowers and tiny trees grow on top of it.

**MINA:** The map was real!

**THEO:** And the island is real!

Piko hops excitedly. A soft gust lifts the map, which floats down and lands open in Mina’s hands.

---

### Scene 5 — The Next Clue

The map redraws itself. The floating island is now marked with a bright blue dot. A second dot appears far away, near a picture of a giant open book.

**THEO:** Is that another place?

Mina looks toward the city library.

**MINA:** I think it’s showing us where to go next.

The map adds one final little drawing: a smiling face wearing glasses.

Piko tilts its head.

**THEO:** Whoever that is, I hope they like visitors.

The silver seed gives one more tiny wiggle. A new line appears on the map, leading from the library toward a place not shown before.

**MINA:** Wait… there’s more!

**Visual:** The map glows. The three friends exchange excited looks as the camera pulls back to reveal the bright city and the floating island above it.

**SFX:** *TWINKLE!*

**END CARD:** *What will they find at the library?*
